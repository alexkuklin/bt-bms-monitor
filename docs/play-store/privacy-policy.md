# Privacy Policy — BT BMS Monitor

_Last updated: 2026-11-01_

BT BMS Monitor ("the app") reads data from a JBD / Xiaoxiang battery management system (BMS) over Bluetooth Low Energy and displays it on your phone.

## Data we collect
**None.** The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not use the internet.

## Data stored on your device
- Alert thresholds and the last-used BMS device address are saved locally on your phone so the app can remember your settings.
- Battery readings (voltage, current, temperature, state of charge) are shown live and kept in memory only for the in-app graph. They are discarded when the app is closed.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

## Permissions
- **Bluetooth (scan / connect)**: to find and communicate with your BMS. The app does not derive your location from Bluetooth scanning.
- **Location (Android 11 and older only)**: required by those Android versions for Bluetooth scanning. It is not used to determine or store your location.
- **Notifications and foreground service**: to keep the connection alive in the background and show battery status and alerts.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
